What is a context hint?

Text Whisper reads before it listens (its initial_prompt). It is not an instruction: Whisper treats it as "what was said just before", so it boosts the exact words and spellings in it. Put "Kirkcaldy" in the hint and "Kirkcaldy" is far more likely to come out, instead of "Capote".

Why route-based beats a generic list

A good hint is short, exact and relevant. Research suggests roughly 20 to 100 tokens. Our generic list of station names was 205 tokens, mostly stations that are nowhere near your train. A route hint is only the stations on your journey, written like an announcement: "Calling at Ladybank, Markinch, Kirkcaldy, Inverkeithing, Haymarket and Edinburgh Waverley."

How it works

Journey (from, to) → look up the stations on the route once, when boarding (while you have signal) → cache them → every announcement on the trip uses the cached hint, offline. Preset journeys are already cached; a new journey needs a live lookup, which can fail (the demo then says so and transcribes without a hint).
